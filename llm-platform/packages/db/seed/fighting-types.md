# Fighting-Type Pokémon of Kanto

The Fighting-type Pokémon of Kanto embody the spirit of martial arts and physical combat. These disciplined creatures train their bodies to extraordinary levels, developing techniques that channel inner energy into devastating strikes. The Fighting Dojo in Saffron City serves as the headquarters for Fighting-type masters and was once an official Pokémon Gym before losing its status to the rival Psychic-type gym led by Sabrina.

## The Fighting Dojo

The Fighting Dojo in Saffron City is run by a Karate King who trains Fighting-type Pokémon with intense dedication. The dojo lost its official Gym status when the Psychic-type Gym, led by Sabrina, defeated it in a challenge match — an ironic defeat since Psychic types are super effective against Fighting types. Despite losing its official status, the dojo continues to operate and rewards visiting trainers who defeat its practitioners with a choice between Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan.

## Machop, Machoke, and Machamp

Machop is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon that begins training its body from birth. Despite its small stature, Machop can lift and throw objects many times its own weight. It practices all styles of martial arts to strengthen every muscle in its body.

Machoke, its evolution at level 28, develops such immense strength that it wears a power-save belt to restrain its own muscles. Without this limiter, its strength would be uncontrollable and dangerous. Machoke is often employed to assist with heavy labor, moving building materials and helping with construction projects.

Machamp evolves from Machoke via trade and gains two additional arms, giving it four in total. Each arm can throw over 500 punches per second, and its four arms allow it to simultaneously guard against attacks while delivering its own. Machamp has mastered every form of martial art known to humanity and uses different arms for different fighting styles. However, tasks requiring fine motor skills and dexterity — like threading a needle or writing — remain beyond its abilities despite its combat mastery.

Competitively, Machamp is valued for its No Guard ability, which guarantees that all attacks it uses (and those used against it) will hit. This makes Dynamic Punch — which normally has only 50% accuracy but always confuses the target — a 100% accurate move that confuses every time it connects. Combined with Stone Edge, Knock Off, and Bullet Punch for priority, Machamp is a reliable and threatening physical attacker.

## Hitmonlee — The Kicking Pokémon

Hitmonlee is named after martial arts legend Bruce Lee and specializes exclusively in kicking techniques. Its legs have incredible elastic properties, stretching to nearly twice their resting length to deliver kicks from unexpected distances. After battle, Hitmonlee massages its legs to relieve tension and maintain their extraordinary flexibility.

Hitmonlee's signature ability is Limber, which prevents paralysis — essential for a Pokémon whose entire fighting style depends on leg mobility. Its hidden ability, Unburden, doubles its Speed when it consumes or loses a held item. Key moves include High Jump Kick (devastating power but punishing on a miss), Close Combat, Blaze Kick, and Mach Punch for priority.

## Hitmonchan — The Punching Pokémon

Hitmonchan is named after legendary boxer Jackie Chan and focuses on punching techniques. Its fists move so fast they become invisible to the naked eye, and it requires rest intervals between bouts of intense punching — much like a real boxer between rounds. Hitmonchan's spirit is that of a dedicated boxer who never gives up regardless of the situation.

Hitmonchan's signature is the elemental punches: Fire Punch, Ice Punch, and Thunder Punch. This coverage trio allows it to hit a wide range of opponents for super-effective damage. Its Iron Fist ability boosts the power of all punching moves by 20%, making it a natural fit for its combat style. Combined with Drain Punch for recovery and Mach Punch for priority, Hitmonchan is a versatile physical fighter despite its modest base stats.
