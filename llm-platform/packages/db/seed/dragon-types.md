# Dragon-Type Pokémon of Kanto

In Generation I, the Dragon type was the rarest and most coveted typing in the game. Only a single evolutionary line — Dratini, Dragonair, and Dragonite — represented this powerful type, and obtaining them required either extraordinary patience or knowledge of their hidden habitats.

## The Safari Zone and Dragon Rarity

Dratini can be found in the wild in exactly one location in the Kanto games: the Safari Zone's fishing ponds. Even there, encounters are extremely rare, requiring the Super Rod and considerable luck. An alternative method involves purchasing a Dratini from the Game Corner in Celadon City for 2,800 coins (later changed to 4,600 in some versions), though this requires a significant investment of time or money at the slot machines.

The extreme rarity of Dragon types in Generation I was a deliberate design choice. Dragon-type moves were limited to just one attack — Dragon Rage — which always dealt a fixed 40 HP of damage regardless of stats. This scarcity gave Dragons a mythical quality that made finally obtaining one feel like a genuine achievement.

## Dratini — The Dragon Pokémon

Dratini is a serpentine, pure Dragon-type Pokémon that was long considered a mythical creature before a small colony was discovered living in an undersea cave. It is born measuring about 5 feet 11 inches in length and grows continuously by shedding its skin repeatedly. The discarded skins can sometimes be found draped over trees near the water bodies where Dratini colonies live.

Dratini possesses the Shed Skin ability, which gives it a chance to naturally cure status conditions each turn. Its hidden ability, Marvel Scale, boosts Defense when afflicted by a status condition. Key early moves include Wrap, Thunder Wave, Dragon Rage, and Slam. Dratini evolves into Dragonair at level 30.

## Dragonair — The Dragon Pokémon

Dragonair is an elegant, serpentine Pokémon revered for its mystical ability to control the weather. The crystalline orbs on its neck and tail radiate a gentle aura that can alter atmospheric conditions, summoning rain, snow, or sunshine at will. Farmers in some regions once worshipped Dragonair as a deity of harvest, praying for it to bring rain during droughts.

Despite its ethereal beauty, Dragonair is a formidable battler in its own right, though most trainers focus on training it to reach its powerful final evolution. It learns valuable moves like Dragon Dance (which boosts Attack and Speed simultaneously), Aqua Tail, and Outrage as it levels up. Dragonair evolves into Dragonite at level 55 — one of the highest evolution levels in Generation I.

## Dragonite — The Dragon Pokémon

Dragonite undergoes a dramatic transformation upon evolution, shifting from Dragonair's sleek serpentine form to a bulky, winged, orange dragon. Despite its intimidating appearance, Dragonite is known for its gentle and altruistic nature. It circles the globe continuously, and sailors have reported being rescued from storms and shipwrecks by this benevolent creature. Dragonite can fly at speeds exceeding Mach 2, allowing it to circumnavigate the entire planet in approximately 16 hours.

Dragonite's base stats are exceptional, with a massive 134 Attack stat and well-rounded defenses. Its hidden ability, Multiscale, halves the damage it takes when at full HP, giving it a crucial survival advantage when setting up. This makes the Dragon Dance + Multiscale combination one of the most feared sweeping strategies in competitive Pokémon.

Its movepool is among the broadest in the game, including Outrage, Extreme Speed (a priority move), Earthquake, Fire Punch, Thunder Punch, Ice Punch, Superpower, and Hurricane. Dragonite can also serve as a special attacker with Draco Meteor, Thunderbolt, and Flamethrower. Lance, the champion of the Kanto Elite Four, is famous for using a team centered around multiple Dragonite.
