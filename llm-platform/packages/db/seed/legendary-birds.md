# The Legendary Birds of Kanto

Three legendary avian Pokémon roost in hidden locations across the Kanto region. Each commands dominion over a fundamental force of nature, and ancient mythology holds that their conflict must be kept in balance lest catastrophe befall the world.

## Articuno — The Freeze Pokémon

Articuno is an Ice/Flying-type legendary Pokémon that dwells deep within the Seafoam Islands, a frozen cave system carved by centuries of glacial water. Its body radiates an aura of freezing cold, causing moisture in the air to crystallize into snow wherever it flies. Articuno's wings are said to be composed of ice so pure that they shimmer with an ethereal blue glow.

This majestic bird appears to doomed travelers lost in icy mountains, guiding them to safety. Despite its benevolent reputation, Articuno is a fearsome combatant. Its signature move, Sheer Cold, can instantly freeze an opponent solid if it connects. Other key attacks include Ice Beam, Blizzard, and Mind Reader. Its ability, Pressure, forces opponents to expend more energy (PP) with every attack they use.

In the Galarian form introduced in later generations, Articuno becomes Psychic/Flying, losing its ice powers in favor of telekinetic abilities. It fires psychic beams from its eyes and can freeze opponents in place using pure mental force.

## Zapdos — The Electric Pokémon

Zapdos is an Electric/Flying-type legendary Pokémon that inhabits the abandoned Power Plant east of Cerulean City. Its body crackles with electrical energy, and each flap of its jagged wings produces bolts of lightning. Zapdos is said to appear when thunderclouds blanket the sky, drawing power from storms to amplify its already devastating electrical attacks.

Zapdos is widely considered the strongest of the three legendary birds in competitive battling, thanks to its excellent Speed and Special Attack stats. Its signature move, Thunder, can strike with devastating accuracy during rain. Combined with moves like Thunderbolt, Drill Peck, and Heat Wave, Zapdos is an incredibly versatile offensive threat. Its Pressure ability, like its siblings, drains opponents' move PP at double the normal rate.

The Galarian form of Zapdos trades its electric powers for Fighting-type energy, becoming Fighting/Flying. This variant cannot actually fly but instead sprints across the land at blinding speed, delivering kicks with enough force to topple buildings.

## Moltres — The Flame Pokémon

Moltres is a Fire/Flying-type legendary Pokémon encountered atop Victory Road, the treacherous path leading to the Pokémon League. Its entire body is wreathed in flames, and each wingbeat scatters embers across the sky. Ancient legends claim that the arrival of Moltres signals the coming of spring, as its fire melts winter snows and awakens dormant plant life.

In battle, Moltres uses its mastery of flame to unleash attacks like Fire Blast, Heat Wave, and Overheat. It also has access to recovery moves like Roost, which allows it to heal mid-battle by landing and resting momentarily. This combination of offensive fire power and self-sustaining recovery makes Moltres a durable special attacker.

The Galarian Moltres is a Dark/Flying type, its flames replaced by an aura of malevolent dark energy. Rather than burning opponents, it drains their willpower, leaving them too demoralized to fight. This sinister variant uses moves like Fiery Wrath, which channels its dark flames to strike all opponents and can cause them to flinch in fear.
