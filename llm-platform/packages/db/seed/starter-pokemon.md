# Starter Pokémon of Kanto

The Kanto region offers new trainers a choice between three remarkable Pokémon, each representing one of the classic elemental types. This decision shapes the entire journey through the Indigo League.

## Bulbasaur — The Seed Pokémon

Bulbasaur is a dual Grass/Poison-type Pokémon, identifiable by the large plant bulb growing on its back. From birth, this symbiotic bulb absorbs sunlight and converts it into energy, allowing Bulbasaur to go for days without eating. As it grows, the bulb on its back develops, eventually blooming into a massive flower when it reaches its final evolution.

Bulbasaur evolves into Ivysaur at level 16, and then into Venusaur at level 32. Venusaur's flower can absorb solar energy and channel it into devastating attacks like Solar Beam. The species is known for its calm and loyal temperament, often serving as a guardian for younger Pokémon.

Key moves include Vine Whip, Razor Leaf, Sleep Powder, and Solar Beam. In competitive battling, Venusaur thrives as a bulky special attacker that can set up Sunny Day to power its Chlorophyll ability, doubling its speed in sunlight.

## Charmander — The Lizard Pokémon

Charmander is a Fire-type Pokémon with a flame that burns at the tip of its tail. This flame is an indicator of Charmander's health and emotional state — it burns brightly when the Pokémon is healthy and flickers when it is weakened. If the tail flame were to go out, Charmander would perish, making this one of the most vulnerable yet spirited starter Pokémon.

Charmander evolves into Charmeleon at level 16, gaining a more aggressive temperament. At level 36, it becomes Charizard, sprouting wings and gaining the Flying type. Despite its draconic appearance, Charizard is classified as Fire/Flying rather than Dragon-type in Generation I. Mega Evolution later gave it two forms: Mega Charizard X (Fire/Dragon) and Mega Charizard Y (Fire/Flying with enhanced special attack).

Signature moves include Flamethrower, Fire Blast, Dragon Claw, and the overwhelmingly powerful Blast Burn. Charizard's versatility in battle has made it one of the most iconic and competitively relevant Pokémon across generations.

## Squirtle — The Tiny Turtle Pokémon

Squirtle is a Water-type Pokémon that withdraws into its rounded shell for protection. Its shell is not merely a defense mechanism — it is hydrodynamically designed to reduce water resistance, allowing Squirtle to swim at remarkable speeds. The shell also serves as a surface for ricocheting attacks in its signature move, Rapid Spin.

Squirtle evolves into Wartortle at level 16, developing a fluffy tail that aids in aquatic balance. Its final evolution, Blastoise, features twin high-pressure water cannons mounted on its shell, capable of blasting jets of water with enough force to punch through thick steel. Mega Blastoise concentrates all its firepower into a single massive cannon on its back.

Key moves include Water Gun, Hydro Pump, Ice Beam, and Skull Bash. Blastoise excels as a defensive wall in competitive play, using Rain Dance to boost its Water-type attacks while supporting its team with moves like Rapid Spin to clear hazards.
